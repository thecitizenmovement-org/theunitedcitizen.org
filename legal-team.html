Paralegal & Legal Team

Legal support for citizens, by citizens — so members are not alone when they face a filing, a deadline, or a courtroom.

What the team is

The Paralegal & Legal Team is the movement’s legal support arm. It works in plain language: it researches the law code, drafts complaints and motions, organizes evidence, and helps members stick to a timeline — so they can escalate lawsuits, court filings, and demands with confidence.

In our membership now

Paralegals — studying now

Our paralegals are members who are studying to become paralegals. They are learning the craft and putting it to work for the movement at the same time.

What they do

  • Research law code and case law for members’ matters.
  • Draft and organize complaints, motions, declarations, and filings.
  • Keep evidence, records, and timelines in order.
  • Help members prepare for hearings and deadlines.

Coming soon

White hat lawyers

White hat lawyers are attorneys who align with the movement’s mission: holding corrupt public servants accountable through the law, and helping ordinary citizens use the legal system to defend their rights.

They are coming soon. The team is in conversation with attorneys who want to stand with citizens, not against them.

What they will do

  • Review and advise on members’ cases and filings.
  • Stand with citizens in court where the law allows.
  • Help the movement hold public officials to the standard the law already sets.

If you are an attorney and you want to stand with citizens, contact the Founder.

How the team helps members

  • Research — find the law code and the case law that applies to your matter.
  • Drafting — help you write complaints, motions, declarations, and public-records requests.
  • Organization — keep your evidence, records, and timeline in order, so nothing is lost and nothing is late.
  • Escalation — help you move a matter forward, from a complaint, to a filing, to a demand, to a courtroom.

Members can reach the team through the Legal Assistant in the store, or by contacting the Founder directly.

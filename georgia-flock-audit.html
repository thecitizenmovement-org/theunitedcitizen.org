Our investigation into the arrests of Joseph Marquee Callaway, Coznavian Stubbs, and Toni Cierra Lewis reveals a calculated exploitation of the Flock Safety system.

These individuals did not merely "misuse" a tool; they bypassed investigative safeguards to conduct off-duty, unauthorized surveillance of private citizens, including the tracking of ex-partners. This is the ultimate breach of the digital perimeter. By weaponizing real-time vehicle location data for personal vendettas, these officers transformed state power into a tool for domestic terror.

The Bibb County incidents highlight the following specific failures of character and professional conduct:

  • Predatory Exploitation of State-Sanctioned Databases: The willful use of non-public surveillance data to satisfy personal obsessions rather than legitimate criminal investigations.
  • Total Collapse of Supervisory Oversight: A failure at the command level to flag highly irregular search patterns and off-duty access logs.
  • Fabrication of Investigative Necessity: The deceptive entry of "official" justifications to mask the tracking of innocent individuals.
  • Willful Violation of Privacy Jurisprudence: Treating the Fourth Amendment as a technicality to be circumvented for private gain.

The fundamental contract between the state and the citizenry rests upon the Oath of Office. In Georgia, every officer swears to "faithfully and impartially discharge and execute all the duties... according to the laws and the Constitution."

When Callaway, Stubbs, and Lewis used Flock cameras to stalk victims, they didn't just break the law; they tore up their oaths. Contrast the solemn promise of "impartiality" with the documented reality of "personal surveillance." One is a commitment to public service; the other is the behavior of a state-sponsored predator.

This systemic failure is driven by specific Institutional Governance Gaps:

  • Zero-Validation Access: Systems currently allow plate searches without requiring a verified, active case number or secondary supervisor approval.
  • Absence of Immutable Audit Trails: Current logs can be obfuscated or ignored by internal "favor" systems, preventing true accountability.
  • Deferred Consequence Model: Misuse is only discovered after a victim files a report, rather than being caught by automated system flags in real-time.

5. The Technological Solution: Continuous and Automated Auditing

The era of "internal investigations" and "self-policing" is over. It has failed. As an expert civil liberties auditor, I assert that the only path forward is the implementation of continuous, automated, and independent audits.

We must move beyond policy memos and into technical requirements:

  • 1. Immutable Logs: Access logs must be stored on a blockchain or a similar write-once, read-many (WORM) storage system to prevent tampering.
  • 2. Cryptographic Justification: Every search must require a cryptographically hashed case number that is cross-referenced with a central dispatch system in real-time.
  • 3. Automated Anomaly Detection: Systems must be programmed to trigger immediate alerts to an independent citizen oversight board when an officer searches for vehicles unrelated to their current geographic assignment or case load.

Call to Action: We demand that the Georgia General Assembly mandate a "Transparency Protocol" for all surveillance vendors. No municipality should be permitted to use Flock Safety technology unless it includes an automated, third-party audit interface accessible to citizen oversight committees. Privacy is not a gift from the government; it is a right we are reclaiming.

Declaration 1 · Friday, November 13, 2026 · Day 132

This war is already over.

War is already hard enough. What you will find about war, oftentimes, is that it is difficult to switch off the battle. If you’re not careful, you can stay in battle until the end of your days.

When your opposition begins to run, hide, and try to ignore your existence, it may be possible that what they’re looking for is a retreat or a surrender. There will be no retreat. Surrender may be an option.

The only way for the digging to stop, and the only way for the cause to stop chasing and battling, is for a surrender to occur.

As a general of over 20 years in the battlefield, one thing I can tell you is this: this war is already over. There is only one option for the opposition, and that is absolute surrender.

The movement demands that you release your political prisoners.

And if you don’t surrender now to your local institution for detainment, the digging and the exposure will continue.

Friday the 13th 2026
Joshua Caleb Scott

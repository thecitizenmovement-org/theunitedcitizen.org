Investigation 13 — Tehama Rashell Ward Murder (1983)

Should the Ward murder questions be reopened?

Rashell Ward, 14, was murdered in 1983. DNA named Johnny Lee Coy in October 2023, and the case was cleared by exception because he died in 2019. But nobody has seen the evidence, nobody has seen any proof, and we’re not satisfied. The open questions: what came back from Koenig’s 1985 DOJ referral, whether Koenig was actually cleared, whether the rumor names Ward, the Lucas confession, and the babysitting claim.

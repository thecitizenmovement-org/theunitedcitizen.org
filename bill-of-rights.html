THE UNITED CITIZEN MOVEMENT & UNITED CITIZEN AMERICA

Bill of Rights & Constitutional Principles for an Audited America

Authored & Promulgated by Joshua Scott, Founder
A Solemn Declaration of Civic Integrity, Truth, and Continuous Oversight

PREAMBLE: We, the Sovereign Citizens of the United States of America, in order to protect the structural integrity of our Republic, guard our inherent civil liberties, restore truth to governance, and ensure that public power remains forever answerable to the governed, do hereby establish and declare these Non-Negotiable Constitutional Principles. An educated, vigilant, and active citizenry is the terminal bulwark against arbitrary authority, state secrecy, and high-tech encroachment upon individual liberty.

ARTICLE I: FREEDOM FROM UNLAWFUL STATE SURVEILLANCE

Every citizen possesses an inviolable constitutional right under the Fourth Amendment to be secure in their person, papers, communications, and movements against unauthorized state tracking. No public officer or agency shall deploy automated license plate readers, database search tools, or artificial intelligence surveillance systems to track, monitor, or profile citizens without a sworn judicial warrant founded upon probable cause of specific criminal conduct.

ARTICLE II: MANDATORY AUTOMATED AUDITING OF STATE POWER

Because power without continuous accountability breeds inevitable corruption, every digital surveillance system, criminal history database, and official search tool operated by state or local authorities must be tethered to mandatory, real-time algorithmic audit tools. Any query that deviates from an active, verified criminal investigation must automatically generate an immutable alert routed immediately to independent oversight bodies.

ARTICLE III: ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION OF PERSONAL ABUSE & SECRECY

Public office is a public trust, not an instrument for personal gratification, stalking, or harassment. Any officer who weaponizes state surveillance tools or official databases against romantic partners, family members, or private acquaintances shall suffer mandatory revocation of law enforcement certification, immediate termination, and full criminal prosecution for oath violation and stalking.

ARTICLE IV: PUBLIC DATA TRANSPARENCY & CITIZEN ACCESS

The public retains the inherent right to inspect, verify, and audit the operational logs, database access records, and internal disciplinary proceedings of all tax-funded law enforcement and administrative bodies. Secrecy in official policy and technology is fundamentally incompatible with a free Republic.

ARTICLE V: EQUAL PROTECTION & DUE PROCESS OF LAW

No citizen shall be deprived of liberty, reputation, or property through fabricated charges, false imprisonment, or malicious state tactics. When government agencies or officials violate civil rights under color of law, injured citizens shall have direct access to legal remedies, administrative redress, and full judicial review under federal civil rights protections.

ARTICLE VI: CIVIC VIGILANCE & THE DUTY TO CORRECT MANAGEMENT

When government management fails to preserve constitutional standards, mismanages public resources, or hides behind ill concepts of authority, it becomes the affirmative right and duty of the citizenry — the true owners of this Republic — to organize, audit, and correct that management through lawful, principled civic action.

ARTICLE VII: GENERATIONAL STEWARDSHIP OF FREEDOM

Freedom is passed undiminished from generation to generation only through unyielding commitment to truth, courage, and unity. The United Citizens Movement and United Citizen America stand united to ensure that no citizen suffers isolation in the face of institutional wrongdoing, and that liberty endures for all generations to come.

SOLEMNLY DECLARED AND ISSUED BY:
Joshua Scott
Founder, The United Citizens Movement & United Citizen America

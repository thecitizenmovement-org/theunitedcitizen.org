The framework: Ministers of Justice and Private Prosecutors

The founder's framework is private grand-jury presentment and private prosecution, as set out in his "Ministers of Justice and Private Prosecutors" work. The citizen brings the evidence to the grand jury directly, rather than waiting on a prosecutor to act.

  • Strongest hook: 18 U.S.C. § 3332 — the statute the framework relies on for a private citizen's access to the grand jury.
  • "Notice to grand jury clerk" — the companion document, giving notice to the clerk of the grand jury.
  • The framework argues from the founder's own documents first, then the honest state of the law.
This is the founder's framework, presented as his position. It is not legal advice, and it is not a filing. Questions of who may prosecute, and under what authority, are attorney questions.

Grand jury clerk complaints — preparation

Clerk complaint templates are prepared in two forms — civil and criminal — and held ready. They are preparatory only.

  • Civil clerk complaint template — prepared, not filed.
  • Criminal clerk complaint template — prepared, not filed.
  • The trigger for any filing is reserved for stonewalling: a clerk refusing to accept or forward a presentment. No clerk has refused anything yet.
What full assistance means here: preserving evidence, keeping the who-did-what record, and drafting reporting materials. It does not mean filing suits, building criminal cases, or threatening anyone with criminal liability.

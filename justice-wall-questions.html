The Justice Wall — Questions Sheet

The United Citizens of America — standard intake for every new Justice Wall case. Use this sheet from now on.

Ask these questions of the family member or referrer. Write down exactly what they say, in their own words. Leave a blank blank — never invent an answer, never fill a gap from a guess. This sheet is the intake. The placard and the case file are built from it only after verification and the founder's approval of the exact change.

Your answers stay on your device until you press Send — nothing on this page stores them. Press Send to open your email with your answers addressed to the founder, or print this sheet and send it another way.

The rules for every case:
  • Your loved one is named, always. The living stay redacted unless the person consents to be named. Minors are never identified.
  • Placement requires excessive force or a deliberately overboard death or injury. Not every officer-involved shooting qualifies.
  • A case is placed only after it is verified.
  • Each case carries its own record: the official account, the family and witness accounts, and why the case earned its place.
  • The wall preserves the record for inquiry. It does not declare guilt or innocence.
  • No words between us on the page. No quoted words of the founder. No profanity. Sources read “Verified sources” only.
  • Nothing goes live until the founder approves the exact change first. The family’s material goes live only by the family’s own choice.
